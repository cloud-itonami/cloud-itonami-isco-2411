# Business Model: Independent Accounting Practice

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-2411`
- ISCO-08: `2411`
- Occupation: Accountants
- Social impact: local-jobs, financial-transparency, small-business-support

## Customer

- small businesses
- sole proprietors
- NPOs

## Offer

- bookkeeping and reconciliation
- financial statement preparation
- tax-filing support
- reporting

## Revenue

- monthly bookkeeping fee
- per-filing fee
- reporting package

## Trust Controls

- no tax filing submission or fund transfer without governor gate
- financial records are auditable, not editable
- client fund access always requires human sign-off
